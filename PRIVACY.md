# Privacy Policy — Sift

**Last updated: 27 August 2026**

Sift does not collect anything.

## What the app stores

Your notes are written as plain Markdown files inside the app's own storage on
your device, along with a version history for each note. Nothing is uploaded.

## What the app sends

Nothing. Sift has no server, no account system, no analytics, no advertising,
no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs that transmit data. The app makes
no network requests of any kind.

The only time data leaves the app is when you deliberately export or share a
note, in which case it goes wherever you choose to send it via the iOS share
sheet or clipboard.

## Face ID

If you lock a note, iOS performs the Face ID or passcode check. The result is a
yes or no. Sift never receives or stores biometric data.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a note moves it to the trash, where it stays for 30 days before being
removed permanently; you can empty the trash immediately from the trash view.
Deleting the app removes every note, every version, and all settings from your
device. Because none of it was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing else to
delete.

## Children

Sift is safe for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.
